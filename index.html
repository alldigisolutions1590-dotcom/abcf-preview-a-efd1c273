02The Shop

Family-owned, woman-owned, with more than eighty years of flowers behind the same bench in Long Beach.

A Beautiful California Florist is a family-owned and operated, minority woman-owned business. The shop has been Long Beach's florist for over eighty years — weddings and quinceañeras, hospital rooms and funerals, offices and front doors across the city.

Nothing is pulled off a shelf. Tell us the colours or the flowers you have in mind and the arrangement is built for the occasion and the room it is going into. More than two hundred designs are listed online; the bench makes them to order.

03How it works

From the market
to your door

Three steps, every day the shop is open.

i.

Chosen each morning

Flowers come in fresh from the Los Angeles Flower Market, and the bench works from what is best that day.

ii.

Made in your size

Standard, Deluxe or Premium — built to order, then photographed so you see the arrangement before it goes.

iii.

Delivered by our drivers

Same-day across Long Beach and next-day on most listings: homes, hospitals, offices and venues.

The shop also offers before-noon delivery, a two-hour rush, Ferrero Rocher boxes, mylar balloons and full-size greeting cards. Those add-ons are not part of this demonstration's checkout.

Custom arrangements, weddings and standing orders: ask the shop, or write below.
